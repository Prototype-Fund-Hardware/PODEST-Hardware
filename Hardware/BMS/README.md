# Battery Management System (BMS)

## Overview

The PODEST Battery Management System (BMS) uses the open-source BMS C1 platform developed by Libre Solar and contributes to its ongoing development.

One of the key goals of the PODEST project is to strengthen existing open-source energy technologies. Instead of developing a new BMS from scratch, the project deliberately builds upon the mature and actively maintained Libre Solar BMS C1 platform. This allows development efforts to focus on improving documentation, testing, integration, and long-term maintainability rather than duplicating functionality that already exists within the open-source ecosystem.

The Libre Solar BMS C1 provides a proven hardware and software platform with active development, extensive field experience, and a growing user community. By collaborating with Libre Solar, PODEST contributes to an existing open-source project while ensuring compatibility with a broader ecosystem of open energy technologies.

Within PODEST, the BMS serves as the central component for battery monitoring, protection, and communication with the overall energy management system. Particular focus is placed on system integration, validation, documentation, and the evaluation of the platform in decentralized renewable energy systems and battery storage applications.

This approach reflects a fundamental principle of open-source development: advancing existing solutions through collaboration and shared knowledge rather than creating parallel implementations.

### Upstream Project

The hardware platform used in PODEST is the Libre Solar BMS C1:

- BMS C1 Documentation: https://libre.solar/hardware/bms-c1.html
- Hardware Repo: https://github.com/LibreSolar/bms-c1
- Firmware Repo: https://github.com/LibreSolar/bms-firmware

## Goals

- [ ] Improve hardware and firmware documentation
- [ ] Validate the hardware through laboratory and field testing
- [ ] Simplify firmware flashing and configuration
- [ ] Simplify battery pack parameterization for different battery systems
- [ ] Integrate the BMS into the complete PODEST energy system
